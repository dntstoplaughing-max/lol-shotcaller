## 2026-08-20 — 27/3/3 comeback win (Zoe mid, Samira bot, Kayn enemy jungle)

Context: behind early — first kill around 7:00 — then turned it around on
discipline: no vulnerability windows, punished Zoe on her cooldowns, ulted
Samira before she could react with W, held ambush positions in brush between
their base and the objectives once ahead, farmed hard throughout. 3 deaths
total. Not in `games.jsonl`: the app was closed mid-game (predates
Ctrl+Alt+H).

1. **Tempo.** Struck a good balance between hesitation and fast play this
   game — but there's another gear: still overthinking a few moments, and
   the overthought moments were where the mistakes lived, not the fast ones.

2. **Level 3: full clear over a possible bot dive** (likely double kill).
   Passed on the dive because it wasn't clear the team would stay and push
   the wave afterward. Own verdict: defensible — full clear always pays
   off. Open question flagged for next time: what would the lane have to
   show (health states, wave position, pings) to make that dive the call?

3. **Escape pathing vs. Kayn — the one unnecessary death.** Ran toward
   base, which meant running at the wall Kayn was in, instead of into the
   enemy jungle where he couldn't chase with E already spent. Cue: pick the
   escape direction by the chaser's remaining mobility, not by the
   direction of home — Kayn without E can't follow through walls.

4. **Flash tracking: none at all.** Attacking too fast for a flash reaction
   worked this game (and stays good general play), but knowing who actually
   had flash would have turned close kills into easy ones — the whole shape
   of duels changes with that information. Cue: note burned flashes on the
   targets being hunted; a burned flash is a kill window until it's back up.
